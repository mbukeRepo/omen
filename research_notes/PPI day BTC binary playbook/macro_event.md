## Q3. What PPI surprise magnitude shifts the Fed narrative; does the gasoline-driven CPI change how PPI is read?

### Takeaway
Because everyone already knows energy was the CPI culprit, a hot PPI *headline* (0.6–0.7%) is largely pre-forgiven; the narrative-moving line is core/ex-trade: ≥0.45% ex-food/energy/trade is "a problem" and ≥0.5% makes an October hike "close to a lock," while headline ≥0.8% or core ≤0.2% are the tail triggers. PPI also matters beyond optics because its components feed September core PCE, the Fed's target metric.

### Cited Findings
- Hot thresholds: ex-food/energy/trade ≥0.45% m/m = "a problem"; ≥0.5% m/m with firm portfolio management, airfares and healthcare components → October hike "close to a lock"; headline ≥0.8% m/m flagged as bad (energy-driven) and would hit the 2-year yield — [CMS Investments](https://cmsinvestments.substack.com/p/building-september-ppi-before-it).
- In-line: headline 0.5–0.6% with core near 0.3% is "not bad" and likely enough for a relief bounce — [CMS Investments](https://cmsinvestments.substack.com/p/building-september-ppi-before-it).
- Soft path: falling trade margins could drag the headline lower, but the market would look through that to the core measures — [CMS Investments](https://cmsinvestments.substack.com/p/building-september-ppi-before-it).
- Market tell: 2-year yield above 4.93% would signal October is priced "as done" — [CMS Investments](https://cmsinvestments.substack.com/p/building-september-ppi-before-it).
- Gasoline pass-through is expected to hit PPI harder than CPI: PPI energy "tend[s] to move as much or more than pump prices"; the author's build has energy +6.5% m/m contributing ~0.33pp to the headline — [CMS Investments](https://cmsinvestments.substack.com/p/building-september-ppi-before-it).
- Why gasoline doesn't fully excuse the CPI: core ran 0.3% m/m for (by this count) a third firm month, which "removes the 'pass-through is fading' argument" — energy did the headline damage but core is what matters for policy — [Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/more/inflation/us-cpi-hot-core-266-october-hike-20261015J1352). CPI detail: unadjusted fuel oil +74.2% y/y, gasoline +34.2% y/y — [FXStreet wrap](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/us-sept-retail-sales-and-ppi-in-focus-along-with-corporate-earnings-202610150544); CEA Chair Phelan called it "not a good month" and defended BLS data reliability — same source.
- PCE channel: PPI matters because of its read-through to PCE prices, the Fed's preferred inflation metric — [FXStreet wrap](https://www.fxstreet.com/analysis/us-sept-retail-sales-and-ppi-in-focus-along-with-corporate-earnings-202610150544).
- Conditional framing: if producer prices "echo the CPI upside, the October hike trade hardens fast" — [Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/more/inflation/us-cpi-hot-core-266-october-hike-20261015J1352).
- Retail-sales overlay: an Admiral illustrative scenario (not an attributed analyst call) says headline retail sales ≥+0.6% with a firm control group "could revive talk of an October hike" — [Admiral Markets](https://admiralmarkets.com/analytics/traders-blog/us-retail-sales-september-2026-preview).

### Inferences
- Scenario map for the 12:30 UTC print: HOT = headline ≥0.7–0.8% OR core/ex-trade ≥0.4–0.45% → October hike odds likely through 50–60%, 2y toward/through 4.93%. IN-LINE = headline 0.5–0.6%, core 0.3% → modest relief, October stays a ~35–45% coin-toss into Warsh's evening remarks. SOFT = core ≤0.2% (and/or headline ≤0.3% on trade margins) → gasoline-only narrative wins, October re-prices back toward the pre-CPI ~22%.
- Because PPI energy is expected to overshoot CPI gasoline, a hot headline alone is the least informative outcome; the market reaction function is concentrated in core ex-trade and the PCE-feeding components (portfolio management, airfares, healthcare).

### Gaps
- Only one source (CMS Investments, a substack) provides explicit numeric surprise thresholds; no bank research (GS/BofA/etc.) thresholds were accessible to corroborate.
- No analyst commentary found on how a retail sales control-group miss would interact with a hot PPI on the same print.
